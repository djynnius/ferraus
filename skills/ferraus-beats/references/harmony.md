# Harmony cheat sheet

## Progressions (Roman numerals; lower case = minor)

| Genre / mood | Progression | In A minor / C major |
|---|---|---|
| pop anthem | I - V - vi - IV | C - G - Am - F |
| pop, emotional | vi - IV - I - V | Am - F - C - G |
| dark pop, trap, drill | i - VI - III - VII | Am - F - C - G |
| trap, minimal | i - VI (2 bars each) | Am - F |
| R&B, neo-soul | ii7 - V7 - Imaj7 - vi7 | Dm7 - G7 - Cmaj7 - Am7 |
| lo-fi, jazzy | Imaj7 - vi7 - ii7 - V7 | Cmaj7 - Am7 - Dm7 - G7 |
| house, deep | i7 - iv7 (stabs) | Am7 - Dm7 |
| afrobeats | I - IV - vi - V or I - V - vi | C - F - Am - G |
| rock | I - IV - V, I - bVII - IV | C - F - G, C - Bb - F |
| sad / cinematic | i - iv - VI - V | Am - Dm - F - E |

## Voicing rules

- Keys: close voicings between C3 and C5; the bass plays the root an octave or two below.
- Voice leading: keep common tones, move the other voices by step; avoid parallel jumps of
  every voice.
- Sevenths and ninths: add colour for R&B, lo-fi, house; leave triads plain for rock and
  anthemic pop.
- Pads: whole notes, root-fifth-third spread over two octaves; stabs: short (1/16-1/8),
  on off-beats or syncopated.
- Arpeggios: chord tones in 16ths, up or up-down, 1-2 octaves.

## Bass

- Root on the kick's hits; a passing note into the next chord's root on the last 8th.
- 808s: long notes on the root, slides (pitch bend or overlapping notes) to the next root;
  stay in E1-A1 for weight, octave up for small-speaker presence.
- House: off-beat roots and octaves; funk: syncopated 16ths with ghost notes.

## Melody

- Use chord tones on strong beats, scale tones between; pentatonic is the safe default.
- Leave space: a 2-bar call, a 2-bar answer; repeat the hook with a small change.
- Keep a vocal-style melody within an octave and a half.
